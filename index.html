Settings

The 12.5 × 109 preset is laid out as 37 mm print + 35 mm blank wrap area + 37 mm print.

Positive moves artwork toward the end of the label; negative moves it toward the start. Paper size stays the same.

Shifts artwork within the 144 px printhead; content at an edge may be clipped.

Uses one label of the selected roll. The pattern marks the start/end and three positions across the head.

D11_H has a measured 144-pixel printhead. Wider labels are automatically clamped to that printable width.
